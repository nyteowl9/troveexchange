Built for Trust

The first TCG marketplace where neither party can get burned. Every single time.

01
Buyer Funds Escrow

USDC locked in a smart contract on Base. Neither party can touch it until the card is verified and delivered to the buyer.

02
Seller Ships to Trove

A pre-paid label routes the card to our authentication center. Seller posts a collateral bond — returned in full on completion.

03
Expert Authentication

A certified human authenticator verifies the card matches the listing. Grade, condition, and identity all confirmed on record.

04
Delivery & Settlement

Card ships to buyer with a 72-hour inspection window. On confirmation, USDC releases to seller. Immutable. On-chain. Final.
