Where is my money? (Escrow explanation)
Your USDC is completely safe and locked in a smart contract on the Base blockchain. It cannot be moved by anyone — not the seller, not us — until the specific conditions of your order are met. Think of it as a digital safe deposit box that only opens under the right circumstances. You can verify this yourself by checking the contract address on Basescan.
How to get Base USDC
The easiest way: Create a Coinbase account at coinbase.com, buy USDC, then withdraw to the Base network (not Ethereum — specifically select Base). Coinbase supports Base withdrawals natively. Alternatively, if you have ETH on Base, you can swap ETH → USDC on Uniswap. Our full guide is at troveexchange.com/#wallets.
What happens after auth rejection
When a card is rejected, your full payment stays locked in escrow — it doesn't go anywhere. The seller has 48 hours to dispute the rejection with evidence. If they don't dispute, you receive a full refund automatically. If they dispute, our resolution team reviews both sides and makes a recommendation within 24-72 hours. Either way, your funds are protected throughout.
Seller account suspension appeal
Your account has been temporarily suspended for missing a shipping deadline. During this suspension, your listings are hidden but no other action is taken. If you have evidence that you did ship within the deadline (carrier receipt, tracking confirmation), please reply to this ticket with that documentation and we will escalate your case to our operations team for review. Suspensions are not permanent and can be reversed with valid evidence.
Escalation Guide
When and who to escalate to
Escalate to Dispute Team (D. Roberts)
• User is asking about an active dispute outcome
• Authentication decision is being contested
• Both parties are giving conflicting accounts and you cannot determine fault
• Suspected fraud by either buyer or seller
• Card damage or condition complaint after delivery
Escalate to Operations Manager
• User is requesting a shipping deadline extension
• Seller claims suspension was issued in error
• User is requesting an account review or strike removal
• Listing dispute (seller claiming their listing was wrongly removed)
• Multiple tickets from same user suggesting coordinated issue
Escalate to Owner (for L2 actions)
• Manual refund is clearly needed and dispute team has confirmed
• Escrow funds appear stuck due to technical issue
• User reports their wallet address has changed and funds need rerouting
• Any situation involving direct manipulation of USDC
• Legal or regulatory inquiry — do NOT handle these, escalate immediately
Handle Yourself (L1 Support)
• How to use the platform (onboarding, wallet, USDC)
• Order status questions (use order lookup)
• Tracking number questions
• Account profile updates
• General FAQ questions
• Reassurance about escrow safety
• Responding to resolved ticket follow-ups
