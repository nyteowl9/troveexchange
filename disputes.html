Resolution Guidelines
Standard operating procedures for dispute resolution
Grade Mismatch Disputes
1. Request close-up photos of grade label from both auth and seller. 2. Cross-reference cert number on official grading company website. 3. If cert lookup confirms seller's stated grade, override auth. 4. If cert lookup matches auth's reading, uphold rejection. 5. If cert lookup unavailable, escalate to owner.
Condition Mismatch Disputes
1. Compare buyer photos with listing photos side by side. 2. Review auth inspection notes and photos. 3. Consider: was damage present at listing or occurred in shipping? 4. Shipping damage = insurance claim, hold escrow. 5. Pre-existing undisclosed damage = refund buyer, strike seller.
Wrong Card Received
1. Immediate refund recommendation — no ambiguity. 2. Issue Strike 1 to seller if confirmed. 3. Flag seller account for enhanced monitoring. 4. Request auth photos — auth should have caught this.
Suspected Fraud
1. Do not communicate further with party until owner is notified. 2. Flag account immediately. 3. Escalate to owner with all evidence. 4. Do not make a recommendation — owner decides on fraud cases.
