## 4. Description of Services

Chase Hollow operates a peer-to-peer marketplace for the buying and selling of trading card game ("TCG") collectibles, including but not limited to Pokémon, Magic: The Gathering, and other card games. The Platform provides:

- **Marketplace listings:** Tools for Sellers to list cards for sale and Buyers to browse and purchase listings
- **Blockchain escrow:** Smart contract-based escrow holding Buyer funds in USD Coin ("USDC") on the Base Layer 2 blockchain network during a transaction
- **Authentication services:** Photo-based (Tier 1) and physical (Tier 2) card authentication performed by Chase Hollow staff

## 5. Fees

**5.1 Platform Fee.** Chase Hollow charges a platform fee of 3.0% of the card sale price, deducted from the Seller's proceeds upon transaction settlement. This fee is not charged to the Buyer.

**5.2 Creator Affiliate Fee.** If a transaction was referred by a Chase Hollow Creator affiliate, an additional 0.5% of the card sale price is deducted from the Seller's proceeds and distributed to the referring Creator. **5.3 Authentication Fee.** Buyers pay an authentication fee at checkout:
- Tier 1 Remote Photo Authentication (cards $1–$300): $10 USDC
- Tier 2 Physical Authentication (cards $301–$50,000): $25 USDC

**5.4 Shipping and Insurance.** Buyers pay the cost of shipping and applicable declared-value insurance, calculated at checkout based on carrier rates. All shipping labels are generated by Chase Hollow. Sellers may not use their own labels.

**5.5 Sales Tax.** Chase Hollow may collect and remit applicable sales tax as required by law. Sales tax obligations are determined by the Buyer's shipping address and applicable state law. Chase Hollow reserves the right to begin collecting sales tax at any time upon notice.

**5.6 Seller Bond.** Sellers are required to post a security bond per transaction, held in the escrow smart contract during the transaction period and returned to the Seller upon successful settlement. Bond amounts are determined by Seller tier:
- New Sellers (0–9 sales): 4% of listing price
- Trusted Sellers (10–99 sales): 3%
- Pro Sellers (100–499 sales): 2%
- Elite Sellers (500+ sales): 1%

The bond is not a fee. It is collateral returned to the Seller upon successful completion. The bond is forfeited to Chase Hollow if the Seller loses a dispute, fails to ship within the required timeframe, or the order is cancelled after the Seller has confirmed it.

**5.7 No Hidden Fees.** The fees described above represent the complete fee structure. Chase Hollow will not charge fees not disclosed at checkout.

## 8. Authentication Services

**8.1 Tier 1 — Remote Photo Authentication.** For cards priced $1–$300, Chase Hollow staff review photos submitted by the Seller while the card is in transit to the Buyer. Authentication is conducted asynchronously and does not delay shipping. Authentication results in a pass or fail determination communicated to both parties.

**8.2 Tier 2 — Physical Authentication.** For cards priced $301–$50,000, the card is physically shipped to Chase Hollow's authentication center before delivery to the Buyer. Chase Hollow staff conduct a physical inspection including photo match verification, grade label verification, certification database verification, slab integrity inspection (for graded cards), holographic sticker verification, and condition assessment (for raw cards). Upon passing, Chase Hollow generates a second shipping label to the Buyer.

**8.3 Authentication Standards.** Chase Hollow's authentication services are performed in good faith by trained staff using reasonable industry standards. Authentication is an opinion, not a guarantee. Chase Hollow does not warrant that authenticated cards are free from all defects or that our authentication determinations are error-free.

**8.4 Authentication Failure.** If a card fails authentication at any tier, the Buyer receives a full refund of the escrow amount, the Seller's bond is forfeited to Chase Hollow, and the Seller receives a Strike. Authentication failure determinations are made solely by Chase Hollow and are final, subject only to the appeal process in Section 12.

**8.5 Physical Custody.** For Tier 2 transactions, Chase Hollow accepts temporary physical custody of cards. While in our custody, cards are covered by Chase Hollow's commercial inland marine insurance policy up to the declared value of the transaction. Chase Hollow is not liable for loss or damage exceeding the declared value or beyond coverage provided by that policy. Buyers and Sellers will be notified immediately of any loss or damage occurring while a card is in Chase Hollow's custody.
